# PrimezNews publishing standard

Apply the Google Publisher Policies and AdSense Program policies when changing public code, ad placements, news, RSS drafts, articles or blogs. Review the current policy for the feature being changed.

- Attribute material facts to the reporting, notice or named speaker that supports them. Distinguish political promises, allegations, proposals and operational services. Reserve newsroom confirmation for a fact personally checked by an identified editor.
- For local coverage, verify the event location or affected area. A feed label, dateline, hospital or publisher address alone cannot establish the district. Include the local consequence when the evidence supports it.
- Give readers original reporting, useful context or substantive curation. Review automated drafts before publication. Check sources, rights to text and images, and the last paragraph across the draft, published database row and public page. Preserve existing story URLs when correcting copy.
- For changes affecting ads, check placement on desktop and mobile, clear separation from editorial and navigation controls, eligible page content, and applicable privacy and consent requirements. Exclude low-value or unfinished pages from monetization.
- Verify relevant automated tests and the published result. Only Google can determine approval and ongoing eligibility.

References: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182 and https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/11190248
