Privacy policy

Your privacy matters to us

This policy explains what information PrimezNews collects when you visit our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

If you contact us, submit a story or send a grievance, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number and message. Submissions made through our upload forms may include text, images and contact details.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the time of visit. We use this to keep the site secure and working well.

We use Google Analytics to measure how many unique visitors use the site, from which country, and on which devices (desktop or mobile). Analytics data is aggregated and IP addresses are anonymised as far as the tool allows. This data is also used to report audience figures to government advertising departments when required under the applicable advertisement policy.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to remember your preferred language, saved stories and bookmarks. This information stays on your device. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings. We do not use cookies for advertising profiles unless this policy is updated and you are informed.

Third-party services

The site may load fonts, images or advertisements from third-party services. Those services have their own privacy policies. Please review them before providing information to them.

How we use information

  • To respond to your messages, corrections and grievances
  • To review and publish submitted stories, after editorial checks
  • To keep the website secure, fast and accurate

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only where required by law, to protect the site, or with service providers who work on our behalf under appropriate confidentiality.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to the contact address below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

PrimezNews is not intended for children under 13. Please do not send personal information about children through our forms.
