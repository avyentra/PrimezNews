Winter Punjabi food: tasty and healthy

Sarson da saag, makki di roti, carrots and seasonal fruit — keeping the winter plate balanced.

PNPrimezNews Health Desk15 November 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • Seasonal produce is freshest and cheapest
  • A little ghee or butter, more vegetables
  • Keep drinking water in winter too

Winter is the tastiest season in a Punjabi kitchen. With a little care, the same food can be good for your health too.

What to put on the plate

  • Leafy greens like saag, spinach and methi
  • Carrots, radish, turnip and peas
  • Protein from dal, chickpeas and rajma
  • Seasonal fruit: oranges, kinnow, guava

What to watch

  • Butter, ghee and fried snacks in moderation
  • Jaggery and sweets are still sugar — take care if diabetic
  • A light dinner, two hours before bed
Tip

Even when you feel less thirsty, keep taking water, warm water or soup through the day.

If you have a medical condition, check with a doctor or dietitian before changing your diet.

This article is general information, not medical advice. Take any medicine or treatment only on a doctor’s advice.
