Mental health

6 simple ways to reduce stress

Work, studies or family worries — everyone feels stress. These small habits help keep the mind light.

PNPrimezNews Health Desk16 November 2026 · 4 min read

Key points

  • Talking is one of the best stress relievers
  • Sleep, exercise and phone breaks help
  • Free counselling: Tele-MANAS 14416
In this article
  1. 1. Talk to someone
  2. 2. Breathe slowly
  3. 3–6. Everyday habits

A little stress is normal, but when it stays every day it affects sleep, appetite and relationships. Try these six simple steps.

1. Talk to someone

Sharing what’s on your mind with family, a friend or someone you trust lightens the load.

2. Breathe slowly

Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, out for 6 — repeat five times.

3–6. Everyday habits

  • Walk or do yoga for 20–30 minutes daily
  • Get 7–8 hours of sleep
  • Phone off an hour before bed
  • Don’t use alcohol or drugs to cope — they make it worse
Tip

If sadness lasts more than two weeks or you have thoughts of harming yourself, call Tele-MANAS 14416 (free) or go to the nearest hospital. Asking for help takes courage.

This article is general information, not medical advice. Take any medicine or treatment only on a doctor’s advice.
