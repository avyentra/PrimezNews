The visible portion is the nail plate, resting securely over the vascular nail bed. At the base, hidden beneath the proximal fold, lies the matrix where new cells actively divide and push forward. Surrounding this area is the eponychium and the cuticle, a delicate rim of skin acting as a barrier against dirt, moisture pooling, and microbial invaders. When this barrier is breached through aggressive clipping or environmental desiccation, the entire nail plate suffers, often showing signs such as horizontal ridges, splitting tips, or painful inflammation around the side walls.

Paying attention to mechanical stress, such as opening stiff lids or scraping utensils using the tips of your fingers, allows you to eliminate invisible sources of micro-trauma before they progress into noticeable splits.

Straight trims with gentle rounding at the outer edges keep the corners from catching on clothing fibers or digging into adjacent skin folds.

Trimming or nipping this seal often leads to acute paronychia, an uncomfortable swelling marked by redness, tenderness, and sometimes bacterial discharge. Keeping this barrier intact is the simplest way to support healthy nail growth habits over a lifetime.

Cuticles act as a natural protective seal that shields the new nail tissue from fungal and bacterial infections. Instead of trimming them, gently push them back after a warm bath using a soft damp cloth.

ਪਾਣੀ, ਮੁੜ੍ਹਕੇ ਅਤੇ ਸਫ਼ਾਈ ਲਈ ਵਰਤੇ ਜਾਣ ਵਾਲੇ ਤੇਜ਼ ਕੈਮੀਕਲਾਂ ਦੇ ਲੰਬੇ ਸਮੇਂ ਤੱਕ ਸੰਪਰਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਰਹਿਣ ਨਾਲ ਨਹੁੰਆਂ ਦੀਆਂ ਕੈਰਾਟਿਨ ਪਰਤਾਂ ਵਿਚਲਾ ਬੰਧਨ ਕਮਜ਼ੋਰ ਹੋ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ। ਬਾਹਰਲੀ ਗਰਮ ਹਵਾ ਅਤੇ ਏਅਰ-ਕੰਡੀਸ਼ਨਡ ਕਮਰਿਆਂ ਦੀ ਖੁਸ਼ਕ ਹਵਾ ਵਿਚਕਾਰ ਵਾਰ-ਵਾਰ ਆਉਣ-ਜਾਣ ਨਾਲ ਵੀ ਨਮੀ ਤੇਜ਼ੀ ਨਾਲ ਘਟਦੀ ਹੈ, ਜਿਸ ਕਾਰਨ ਨਹੁੰ ਛਿੱਲੇ ਜਾਣ ਅਤੇ ਟੁੱਟਣ ਲੱਗਦੇ ਹਨ।लंबे समय तक पानी, पसीने और तेज़ डिटर्जेंट के संपर्क में रहने से नाखून के केराटिन की परतों के बीच की पकड़ कमज़ोर पड़ जाती है। बाहर की भीषण गर्मी और अंदर एसी के सूखे माहौल के बीच बार-बार आने-जाने से भी नमी तेज़ी से छिन जाती है, जिससे नाखून छिलने और टूटने लगते हैं।Prolonged exposure to water, sweat, and harsh cleaning agents weakens the bonds between nail keratin layers. Frequent transitions between hot outdoor air and dry air-conditioned rooms can also cause rapid moisture loss, leading to peeling and breakage.
