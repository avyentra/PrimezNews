What you can complain about

  • A news story that is inaccurate, defamatory or unfair to you
  • Content that infringes your privacy or intellectual property
  • Content that is obscene, harmful to minors or otherwise unlawful
  • An advertisement that is misleading or not labelled as sponsored

How to file a complaint

Send an email to the Grievance Redressal Officer with your full name, contact details, the web address (URL) of the content, a clear description of your concern, and any supporting documents. Complaints received without these details may take longer to process.
