EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Clear sources. Fair context.
Visible corrections.

Proposed editorial policy for this design preview. PrimzNews currently displays sample content, not a live verified news service.

Before a story is published

Original sources, relevant documents and the context of photographs should be checked. Claims, opinion and established facts should be distinguished. People facing material allegations should have an opportunity to respond.

Bylines and dates

Published reporting should name its author, show the original publication date and identify substantive updates. Sample articles do not claim a reporting date.

Corrections

Material errors should be corrected visibly, with an explanation of what changed and when. A verified newsroom contact channel must be added before launch; this preview does not accept correction submissions.

Advertisements

Paid display creatives and sponsored content must be clearly labelled and kept distinct from independent editorial coverage.

Images and privacy

Illustrative and generated visuals should be identified appropriately. Sensitive personal details and the dignity of people featured in reporting require editorial review.
