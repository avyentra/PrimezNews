Editorial policy

Clear sources. Fair context. Visible corrections.

Sourcing and verification

Reporting is based on original sources, official documents, first-hand observation and on-record statements wherever possible. Claims are checked before publication. Where a claim cannot be verified, we say so.

Facts, opinion and analysis

News reports state facts. Opinion, analysis and commentary are labelled clearly and kept separate from news reporting.

Right of response

People or organisations facing material allegations are given a fair opportunity to respond before publication, and their response is reported where it is relevant.

Bylines and dates

Published reports name their author or reporting desk and show the publication date. Substantive updates to a story are marked with the date of the update.

Corrections and clarifications

Material errors are corrected visibly, with a note describing what changed and when. Corrections can be sent through our grievance redressal or contact page.

Advertisements and sponsored content

Paid advertisements and sponsored content are clearly labelled and kept separate from independent editorial coverage. Advertising does not influence editorial decisions.

Images and sensitive content

Illustrative or generated images are identified as such. Images and details that could harm the dignity or safety of individuals, including minors and victims of crime, are reviewed before publication.
